# Axioma — Version 2

## At a glance

Version 2 gives the number field a more immersive, polished presentation. The score and rank remain visible, while the controls, color themes, and stats are gathered into panels that are easier to explore on a small screen.

## Playing

Select two numbers that are the same or add up to 10. They can be matched when every number between them in the reading order has been cleared, or when they are in the same column with only cleared spaces between them. Diagonal matches are not allowed.

Clear the whole field to finish the puzzle. When no useful pair is available, deal the remaining numbers again at the end of the field. Dealing becomes available after your first match; new numbers cost points, while completely cleared rows earn points when removed.

## What changed for players

- Choose from a range of color themes instead of a single dark appearance.
- Open the dashboard for a quick view of your score, time, moves, and deals, then expand it for detailed match and board statistics.
- See a completion summary when you clear the field.
- On phones, the game guides you to a comfortable portrait view and may offer home-screen installation instructions for a more immersive play experience.
